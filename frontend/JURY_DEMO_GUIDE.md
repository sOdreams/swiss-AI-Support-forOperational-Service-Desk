# Jury Demo Guide — Human Review Workbench

## 60–90 second story

1. Start with the 20-ticket SwissLife challenge queue, or upload a Jira-style JSON file from the queue panel.
2. Select `TICKET-10` and show the original ticket at the top of the middle workspace.
3. In **Compare and decide**, compare the current Jira value, the AI suggestion and the final decision in one table.
4. In **AI Evidence**, show the case summary, confidence, routing snapshot, reasons and supporting historical sources.
5. Click **Approve**, or choose **Edit** to correct the work type, request type, priority or service team before saving.
6. Show that the original ticket remains unchanged and the review status becomes `Approved`, `Edited & approved` or `Rejected`.
7. In **Record the real outcome**, enter the actual solution, select an affected business aspect and save processing feedback.
8. Select `TICKET-7` to demonstrate the safety path for an unclear, high-priority request: the interface asks the analyst to resolve missing information before accepting the recommendation.

## What this demonstrates

- Queue exploration with search, priority/status filters and sorting.
- Clear separation between original Jira data, AI evidence and the analyst's decision.
- Human-in-the-loop approval: AI suggestions never overwrite source data automatically.
- Editable, reviewable corrections with an audit note.
- Structured processing feedback for later evaluation and improvement.
- Evidence and confidence are visible before a decision is made.
